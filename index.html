Hello! I'm Ankur Barsode

About Me

I am a final-year PhD student at the International Centre for Theoretical Sciences (a centre of the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research) in Bengaluru, India. My research revolves around the fascinating world of gravitational waves, where I explore the intricacies of lensing phenomena and their implications for understanding the universe. Beyond my core research, I am deeply passionate about scientific communication, mentoring the next generation of researchers, and developing efficient computational tools for data analysis.

Research Interests

My primary research focuses on gravitational wave astronomy, with a particular interest in gravitational lensing. I dedicate much of my work to developing searches for strongly lensed gravitational waves and understanding the broader implications of gravitational wave lensing in cosmology and astrophysics.

In addition to these core topics, I am committed to advancing scientific software through machine learning methods to accelerate complex calculations. My work often involves rigorous parameter estimation and the application of Bayesian statistics to extract meaningful insights from observational data.
