Profile

About

I am a final-year PhD student at the International Centre for Theoretical Sciences (ICTS), a centre of the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research. My research centres on gravitational-wave lensing and its potential to deepen our understanding of the universe. Alongside my research, I contribute to scientific software, mentorship, and the communication of gravitational-wave science.

Research programme

Research interests

Gravitational-wave lensing

Developing robust searches for strongly lensed gravitational waves and assessing their scientific implications for astrophysics and cosmology.

Bayesian inference

Designing principled statistical methods for parameter estimation, model selection, and extracting reliable conclusions from observational data.

Scientific computing

Applying efficient numerical and machine-learning methods to accelerate demanding calculations in gravitational-wave data analysis.
